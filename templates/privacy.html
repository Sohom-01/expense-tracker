Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what we need to run the Service:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password (stored in hashed form, never as plain text).
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you choose to enter.
  • Basic technical data — such as session cookies needed to keep you logged in.

Spendly does not connect to your bank accounts and never asks for card numbers, bank details, or other payment information.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used solely to:

  • Create and manage your account and keep it secure.
  • Store your expenses and show you summaries and breakdowns of your spending.
  • Respond to your questions or support requests.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secured database and is accessible only through your account. We take reasonable measures to protect it against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse, though no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal information and expense records will be removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your data with third parties for their own purposes. The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive basic technical information such as your IP address when the page loads. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Your Rights

You can view, edit, or delete your expense data at any time from your account. You may also request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account entirely.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the Spendly website.
